Delete your SUP Cruise data

SUP Cruise: Island Quest · developer Pera Technology (Google Play)

The game has no account, e-mail or password. It only sends data when you join the optional online features (leaderboard, captain races, Harbour Market). You can ask us to delete that data at any time — no reason needed.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Write an e-mail (a parent can write it for a child) to:
    support@supcruisegame.com
    Subject: Delete my SUP Cruise data
  2. Tell us the name of your character(s) exactly as it appears in the game, and roughly when you played online (for example "October 2026"). This is how we find the anonymous online records — we never ask for a real name, address or ID. Even better: copy the player code from Settings (shown under Online leaderboard, tap it to copy) and paste it into the e-mail — then we find the right data for sure.
  3. We reply to confirm, and delete the data within 30 days.

You can also stop sharing right away in the game: Settings → Online leaderboard off. To remove the game data stored on your phone, delete the character in the game (Characters) or uninstall the game.

What is deleted

DataWhereWhen
Character name and look (girl or boy, outfit, hat)Unity Gaming Serviceswithin 30 days of your e-mail
Leaderboard scores and race trophiesUnity Gaming Serviceswithin 30 days

The random anonymous player ID of each characterUnity Gaming Serviceswithin 30 days

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept by us. Your e-mail request is deleted once the deletion is done.
  • Purchases are made and recorded by Google Play, not by us; Google keeps them under its own rules (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Optional video ads come from Google AdMob; we do not receive or store that data (how Google uses data).
  • Game progress saved only on your phone never reaches us; it is removed when you delete the character or uninstall the game.
