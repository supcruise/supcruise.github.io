Privacy Policy

SUP Cruise: Island Quest has no account or sign-in and does not ask for your name, e-mail, photos, contacts or location. The developer does not receive or store any personal information; only the optional leaderboard shares your character's name and scores (see below).

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (islands, gold, fish, outfits, settings) is saved only on your device. If Android Backup is turned on, Android may back up this save file to your own Google account so it comes back on a new phone; this is handled by Google and can be switched off in your phone's settings. We never receive it.

Optional video ads (🎬)

You can choose to watch a short video ad for a reward (for example double gold). Ads only appear when you tap a 🎬 button; the game never shows ads on its own. The ads come from Google AdMob and are set up for a family audience: child-directed, suitable for all ages (rating G), no personalised or interest-based ads and no advertising ID. To show an ad and prevent fraud, Google receives technical information such as the device's IP address (for approximate country/region), device type, and app and ad interactions. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases and subscription

Optional packs (for example the "Captain Pack" or the cosmetic bundles) and the optional monthly "Captain's Club" membership are bought through Google Play. Payment, renewals and cancellation are handled entirely by Google; the game only learns which packs are owned and whether the membership is active. The membership can be cancelled at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions). Purchases can be restored in Settings. Packs only change how the character looks.

Lucky Wheel

The Lucky Wheel is paid with gold earned in the game, never with real money directly. The chance of every prize is shown on the wheel screen.

Photo mode

Photos you take in photo mode are saved only to your own phone's gallery (Pictures/SUP Cruise). They are never uploaded or sent to us. On Android 7–9 the game asks for storage access only to save these photos.

Online leaderboard (optional)

The leaderboard is off until you join it (on the leaderboard screen or with "Online leaderboard" in Settings). When it is on, the game sends your character's name (checked by a word filter), its look (girl or boy, outfit, hat) and its scores to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies), which stores them and shows the best players to everyone. To keep the scores apart, Unity gives each character a random anonymous player ID; there is no account, e-mail or password, and nothing else about you is sent. While a character is online, the game also asks the server which Weekly Adventure runs this week; only the week (for example "2026-W42") is sent and nothing is stored. The service is set up for a child-directed game (COPPA). Each character joins on its own: joining with one character does not put your other characters online. Switching "Online leaderboard" off in Settings (or deleting the character) removes that character's public profile and recorded runs from the server and withdraws its market offers; if the device is offline, this happens the next time it is online. Weekly scores are cleared every Monday; to have the character's all-time score removed too, write to us or use our deletion page: https://supcruisegame.com/delete/ Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Harbour Market (optional)

Players who joined the online leaderboard can trade fish with other players. For a trade, the character's name, look and the fish offered are shared with other players; offers are deleted after 48 hours. There is no free chat — only preset messages can be sent. Open offers are withdrawn when the character leaves the online leaderboard or is deleted.

Captain races (optional)

Players who joined the online leaderboard can race against another captain's recorded run (a "ghost"). For this, your best run on each island stage (only your board's movements — no voice, camera or photos), your character's name and look, and your race trophies are stored on Unity Gaming Services and shown to other players. After a race you can send one of a few preset messages (for example "Great race!"); there is no free chat. Turning "Online leaderboard" off in Settings (or deleting the character) stops this and removes your recorded runs from the server; to have all your data deleted, write to us.

Reporting and hiding names

Next to another captain's name there is a 🚩 button. "Hide this captain" works only on your device and sends nothing. "Report this name" sends us that captain's name, an anonymous code for that captain and an anonymous code for your character (never your own name); we check the report and keep it for at most 30 days. Names we remove (or that several players report) are shown as "A captain" to everyone, and their owner is asked to choose a new name.

Language

To start in the language of your country, the game uses your Google Play country (remembered on your phone only), or else the country of your SIM card, mobile network or phone settings. If the game doesn't have your country's language, it starts in English; you can change the language any time in Settings. This happens on the device only and is never sent anywhere.

Age range (Brazil)

Brazilian law (Digital ECA) asks games that children may play to read the player's age range from the app store. In Brazil only, the game asks Google Play for it; Google Play may first ask you (or your parent) whether to share it. The game receives only a range (for example 0–12), keeps it on your phone and never sends it to us or anyone else. In Brazil the Lucky Wheel is replaced by a Daily Gift whose prizes are known in advance.

Permissions

Vibration (small rumble effects), internet access (only for the optional ads, Google Play purchases and the optional leaderboard) and, on Android 7–9 only, storage access to save photo-mode pictures. No location, camera, microphone or contacts permission.

Children

The game is made for the whole family, including children, and follows Google Play's Families policy. The developer collects no personal data from children. Parents can turn off in-app purchases with Google Play's parental controls.

在你加入之前，排行榜是关闭的（可在排行榜界面或通过设置中的“在线排行榜”加入）。开启后，游戏会把你角色的名字（经过词语过滤器检查）、外观（女孩或男孩、服装、帽子）和分数发送到 Unity Gaming Services（Unity Technologies），由它保存这些信息并向所有人展示最佳玩家。为了区分分数，Unity 会给每个角色分配一个随机的匿名玩家 ID；没有账户、电子邮箱或密码，也不会发送任何其他关于你的信息。角色在线时，游戏还会向服务器询问本周进行的是哪个“本周冒险”；只会发送周数（例如“2026-W42”），不会保存任何内容。该服务按面向儿童的游戏（COPPA）进行设置。每个角色单独加入：用一个角色加入不会让你的其他角色上线。在设置中关闭“在线排行榜”（或删除角色）后，该角色的公开资料和录制的航行记录会从服务器上删除，其在市场上的报价也会撤回；如果设备当时未联网，会在下次联网时完成。每周分数每个星期一清零；如需同时删除该角色的总分，请写信给我们或使用我们的删除页面：https://supcruisegame.com/delete/ Unity 的隐私政策：https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

港湾集市（可选）

加入在线排行榜的玩家可以和其他玩家交换鱼。进行交换时，角色的名字、外观和拿出来交换的鱼会与其他玩家共享；交换请求会在 48 小时后删除。没有自由聊天——只能发送预设消息。当角色退出在线排行榜或被删除时，其未完成的报价会被撤回。

船长比赛（可选）

加入在线排行榜的玩家可以和另一位船长录制下来的航行（“幽灵”）比赛。为此，你在每个关卡中的最佳航行（只有桨板的移动——没有语音、摄像头画面或照片）、你角色的名字和外观，以及你的比赛奖杯，会保存在 Unity Gaming Services 上并展示给其他玩家。比赛结束后，你可以从几条预设消息中选一条发送（例如“好比赛！”）；没有自由聊天。在设置中关闭“在线排行榜”（或删除角色）即可停止这一功能，并会从服务器上删除你录制的航行记录；如需删除你的全部数据，请写信给我们。

举报和隐藏名字

其他船长的名字旁边有一个 🚩 按钮。“隐藏这位船长”只在你的设备上生效，不会发送任何内容。“举报这个名字”会把那位船长的名字、那位船长的匿名代码和你角色的匿名代码（绝不包括你自己的名字）发送给我们；我们会检查举报，并最多保存 30 天。被我们删除的名字（或被多位玩家举报的名字）会对所有人显示为“一位船长”，名字的主人需要选择一个新名字。
