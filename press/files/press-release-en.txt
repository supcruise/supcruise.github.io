SUP Cruise: Island Quest – a kid-safe stand-up paddle adventure, fully voiced in 14 languages, is coming to Google Play

ISTANBUL, Türkiye – Pera Technology today announced SUP Cruise: Island Quest, a sunny stand-up paddle (SUP) adventure for kids and families, coming soon to Google Play.

In SUP Cruise, old One-Eyed Pete has torn his treasure map into pieces and hidden them across the sea. Players team up with Captain Tolo, the friendly lighthouse keeper of Pirate Cove, and paddle across seven story islands – Pirate Cove, Coral Reef, Misty Caves, Volcano Island, Monkey Temple, Storm Cape and the legendary Lost Paradise – to put the map back together.

The controls are simple: swipe to change lanes, jump over wave ramps and duck under branches. Along the way, players guide baby turtles home, race dolphins, calm the monkey guardians with bananas and fly through gates of light. Between the runs they can paddle freely across the open sea and catch 25 kinds of fish, from sardines to the legendary Moonfish. Younger players can switch on auto-paddle.

The whole story is fully voiced in 14 languages: English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Greek, Arabic, Japanese and Chinese.

SUP Cruise was designed for families from the start:
- No free chat – players can only send a few friendly preset messages.
- No account, no e-mail address and no location.
- Online features (leaderboards, races, fish trading) are optional and off by default.
- Ads only play when the player taps the 🎬 button.
- The game plays offline and offers a gentle difficulty and a "Soft glow" option that is easy on the eyes.

"We wanted kids to feel the freedom of the sea, the sun and discovery. We turned stand-up paddling into a treasure hunt and designed everything so that families can relax while their kids play," said Volkan Tarım, founder of Pera Technology.
