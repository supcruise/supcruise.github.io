About the game

Grab your paddle! SUP Cruise: Island Quest is a sunny stand-up paddle (SUP) adventure across seven story islands, made for kids and families.

Old One-Eyed Pete tore his treasure map into pieces and hid them across the sea. Players team up with Captain Tolo, the friendly lighthouse keeper of Pirate Cove, paddle from island to island, uncover their secrets and put the map back together.

Each island – Pirate Cove, Coral Reef, Misty Caves, Volcano Island, Monkey Temple, Storm Cape and the legendary Lost Paradise – has its own story, music and surprises: guide baby turtles home, find a way through glowing caves, calm the monkey guardians with bananas and fly through gates of light. Every island is played in five stages with three stars to earn in each – 35 stages and 105 stars in all – and a new Weekly Adventure starts every Monday. Between the runs, players can paddle freely across the open sea and catch 25 kinds of fish.

One paragraph

SUP Cruise: Island Quest is a family-friendly stand-up paddle adventure for iPhone, iPad and Android. Kids paddle across seven story islands and 35 stages with Captain Tolo to rebuild a lost treasure map, take on a new Weekly Adventure every week, fish on the open sea and create their own captain – fully voiced in 14 languages, playable offline, with no free chat, no account and no location tracking.

Key features

  • 7 story islands, 35 stages – each island has 5 stages with up to 3 stars each (105 stars in all), its own story, music, obstacles, story items to collect and a piece of the treasure map.
  • Weekly Adventure – a new 7-chapter story every Monday; one chapter opens each day and finishing the week earns a special reward. Twelve adventures rotate through the year.
  • Easy to learn, fun to master – swipe to change lanes, jump wave ramps, race dolphins, slingshot out of whirlpools. Younger players can turn on auto-paddle.
  • Open-sea fishing – find bubbling spots and catch 25 kinds of fish, from sardines to the legendary Moonfish; fill the Fish Log.
  • Captain Tolo speaks your language – the whole story is voiced in 14 languages.
  • Create your own captain – choose a look, a name and a special skill (Balance Master or Power Paddle); unlock hats, boards, paddles, pets and wake trails.
  • Optional online play – weekly and all-time leaderboards, races against another captain's best run and fish trading at the Harbour Market. Off by default.

Made for families

  • No free chat – players can only send a few friendly preset messages.
  • No account, no e-mail address, no location.
  • Online features are optional and switched off by default.
  • Ads only play when the player taps the 🎬 button.
  • Plays offline, with a gentle difficulty curve and a “Soft glow” option that is easy on the eyes.

Designed for iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo the phone itself becomes Captain Tolo's treasure map. While it is closed, the cover screen shows his sealed map and Tolo calls the player. Unfolding the phone cracks the seal: the map opens, the seven islands pop up one by one and the adventure starts on the big inner screen. Folding it mid-run pauses the game behind a brass spyglass until the phone is opened again.
