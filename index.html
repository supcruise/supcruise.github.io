Designed for iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo the phone itself becomes Captain Tolo's treasure map. Closed, the cover screen shows his sealed map and Tolo calls you. Unfold it: the seal cracks, the map opens and the islands pop up one by one, then you dive into the adventure on the big inner screen. Fold it mid-run and the game waits behind a brass spyglass until you open it again.

So much to do on the water

🌊

Paddle, jump and dodge

Race the dolphins, fly over wave ramps and bonk sharks on the nose!

🎣

Go fishing

Cast, hold and reel. Catch 25 kinds of fish and fill your Fish Log.

🗺️

Captain Tolo speaks your language

The friendly lighthouse keeper tells the story — fully voiced in 14 languages.

🏆

Race and trade

Race other captains on the leaderboard and swap fish at the Harbour Market.

🛍️

Beach Shop: gear with powers

Boards, hats, glasses and necklaces — every item gives your captain a power. Buy them with the gold you earn while paddling.

⚖️

Harbour Market

Trade fish with captains from all over the world. The brass scale makes sure every trade is fair — only fish are traded.

📜

Weekly Adventure

A new 7-chapter story every Monday! One chapter opens each day — finish them all for the week's special reward.
